YTV privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

YTV is a personal, non-commercial YouTube app for a smart TV. It runs entirely on the TV. It has no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising of its own and no tracking.

What YTV accesses

When you sign in, YTV asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly). It uses that access to read:

  • the list of channels you subscribe to
  • recent public uploads from those channels
  • your YouTube channel name, to show who's signed in

Without signing in, YTV only uses public YouTube data: search results and video details.

Where it's kept

Everything YTV stores stays in the TV's own app storage: your sign-in token, a short-lived cache of your subscriptions and their latest videos, recent searches and your settings (such as playback speed). None of it is sent anywhere except to Google and YouTube, to make the requests above. YTV doesn't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Google and YouTube

YTV uses the YouTube API Services. Videos play in YouTube's official embedded player, and YouTube may collect data under its own policies. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

  • In YTV, Account → Sign out deletes the token and cached data from the TV and asks Google to revoke access.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account's third-party access page.
  • Uninstalling YTV from the TV removes everything it stored.
