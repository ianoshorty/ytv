YTV

A personal YouTube app for a Hisense VIDAA smart TV.

YTV is a small, non-commercial web app that one person built for their own TV. It plays YouTube videos with YouTube's official embedded player, so 60 fps videos stay at 1080p when played at faster speeds. The TV's built-in app drops them to 480p.

What it does

  • Shows the newest videos from the channels you subscribe to
  • Lists your subscriptions and each channel's uploads
  • Searches YouTube
  • Plays videos full screen with speed control from 0.25x to 2x

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account using Google's code sign-in for TVs. YTV asks only for read-only access to your YouTube account, to show your subscriptions. It can't post, like, subscribe, comment or change anything. See the privacy policy for what it reads and where that's kept.

About this project

YTV isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google or Hisense. It uses the YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. It doesn't block or skip ads.
